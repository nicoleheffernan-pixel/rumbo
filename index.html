How it works

From merchant profile to a decision your team owns

The tool predicts; people decide. Every step shows where its numbers come from.

  1. Describe the merchant

    Target markets, industry, ticket size, risk, local entity and, if available, its own payment history.

  2. Learn from similar merchants

    Their real approval rates with each provider become the starting point, adjusted with the merchant's own results.

  3. Compare the options

    Sales, costs and time to go live for each option, compared on first‑year profit.

  4. Decide, then keep watching

    The payments team approves or overrides with a reason. Review alerts reopen the decision when results drift.

Principles

Built to be challenged

Provider‑agnostic

No provider can pay for its ranking. The method is disclosed, so the payments team can check why a setup wins.

Uncertainty on every estimate

Approval estimates come with a range, a sample size and their source. Thin data is flagged instead of hidden.

about 81% (likely 78–85%)Similar merchantsFew similar merchants

People make the call

Contracts, pricing and relationships stay with the Head of Payments, Finance and Legal. Overrides are expected and logged.

Reopened on evidence

Approval drift, a shift in payment-method mix or a better peer benchmark triggers a review, and so does the 6‑month calendar.

What we measure
